About Our Institution

Established 2001

Bengal College of Engineering & Technology (BCET), Durgapur, was established under the aegis of SKS Educational Social Trust in 2001. The college offers top-tier engineering programs in Computer Science & Engineering, AI & ML, Data Science, Electronics & Communication, Electrical, Information Technology, Mechanical, and Civil Engineering.
